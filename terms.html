5. Rewards & Payouts

Reward amounts vary by task type and are shown in the Platform before you complete a task. Typical task types include:

  • Watch a video — a small SOL reward per completed watch
  • Like a post — a small SOL reward per verified like
  • Comment on a post — a larger SOL reward per verified comment
  • Subscribe or follow a creator — a one-time reward, plus an ongoing monthly residual for as long as you remain subscribed

How Payouts Work

  • Payouts are sent directly to your connected Solana wallet — we never hold your funds in a custodial balance beyond what's pending payout
  • CoinDrop charges zero platform fees on payouts
  • Tasks are verified (including automated screenshot verification) before a reward is credited; fraudulent or unverifiable submissions are not paid
  • A cooldown period applies per task per piece of content to prevent repeat-claiming the same task
  • SOL's market value fluctuates; the USD-equivalent value of a reward is not fixed or guaranteed
