Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about earning on CoinDrop

Getting Started

Is CoinDrop really free to join?
Yes. CoinDrop is 100% free — there's no sign-up fee, no subscription, and no minimum investment required to start earning. You just need an account and a Solana wallet to receive payouts.
How do I start earning?
  1. Create a free account
  2. Connect a Solana wallet (we recommend Phantom)
  3. Open the Tasks tab and pick a task — watch a video, like a post, subscribe to a creator, and more
  4. Complete the task and submit verification
  5. Get paid in SOL directly to your wallet
Is CoinDrop available in my country?
CoinDrop is available in 190+ countries worldwide. You do need to be at least 13 years old, and it's your responsibility to confirm that participating is lawful where you live.
Do I need a wallet before I sign up?
No — you can register first and connect a Solana wallet (like Phantom) afterward. You will need a connected wallet before any earnings can be paid out, though, so it's worth setting one up early.

Tasks & Rewards

What kinds of tasks can I complete?
Tasks span YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and X content from participating creators — watching a video, liking a post, leaving a comment, and subscribing or following a channel. New tasks are added regularly as creators join the platform.
How much does each task pay?
Reward amounts vary by task type and are shown on the task card before you start it. As a general pattern: watching pays the least per task, commenting pays more, and subscribing/following pays a one-time reward plus an ongoing monthly residual for as long as you stay subscribed.
How is my task verified?
Most tasks are verified automatically — for example by checking a screenshot you submit against the expected result (like a "subscribed" or "liked" state). This keeps rewards fair for everyone and prevents fraud.
Can I do the same task over and over to earn more?
No — each task has a cooldown period per piece of content, so you can't repeat-claim the same watch, like, or comment on the same video to farm rewards. New content becomes available regularly, so there's always something fresh to earn from.

Payouts

How do I get paid?
Rewards are paid in SOL (Solana) directly to the wallet address connected to your account. There are no platform fees on payouts.
What wallet should I use?
We recommend Phantom, but any standard Solana (SPL-compatible) wallet address will work.
Is the value of my SOL rewards fixed?
No. You're paid in SOL, and SOL's market price moves like any cryptocurrency. The USD-equivalent value of your earnings will fluctuate with the market.

Creators & Referrals

What is the Creator Referral Program?
If you refer a creator to CoinDrop using your unique referral link and they join, you earn a SOL reward for that referral. Full details and your referral link are available from your dashboard.
I'm a creator — how do I get my content on CoinDrop?
Reach out to us at support@illyrobotic-ai.com or join our Discord to get started.

Account & Safety

Is my personal data safe?
We use industry-standard protections including AES-256 encryption for sensitive data. See our full Privacy Policy for details on what we collect and how it's protected.
Can I have more than one account?
No — only one CoinDrop account per person is allowed. Multiple accounts, bots, or automated engagement are against our Terms of Service and may result in forfeited rewards or a suspended account.
What happens if my account is flagged for fraud?
We use automated fraud detection to keep the platform fair. Accounts found violating our Terms (botting, fake engagement, multiple accounts, etc.) may have pending rewards forfeited and the account suspended or terminated.
